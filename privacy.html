Overview

Kairox is a screen-time and movement application designed to block selected apps and let users earn social-media minutes by completing supported exercises.

Information Kairox may process

  • Account information used for sign-in.
  • Selected app restrictions, earned-minute balances, exercise selections, repetition counts, and progress history.
  • Device, diagnostic, crash, notification, and subscription-status information needed to operate the app.

Camera and movement detection

Kairox may request camera access to detect body positioning and count exercise repetitions. The final policy must accurately state whether camera frames are processed on-device, transmitted, retained, or shared.

Family Controls and app blocking

Kairox may use Apple Screen Time, Family Controls, Managed Settings, and related frameworks to identify user-selected apps and apply restrictions. Kairox does not need access to the content inside those apps to restrict them.

How information is used

Information may be used for sign-in, app blocking, repetition counting, earned-minute calculations, progress tracking, notifications, subscription verification, security, diagnostics, and customer support.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Kairox does not directly store complete payment-card information.

Data sharing

Kairox should not sell personal information. Service providers may process limited data to support authentication, infrastructure, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, or subscription management.

Deletion and retention

Users should be able to request deletion of their account and associated data through the app or published support channel, subject to legal and transaction-record requirements.
