Support Center

Kairox helps you block distracting social apps and earn controlled unlock time through push-ups, squats, and lunges.

How do I start?

Create an account, grant the required Screen Time or Family Controls permissions, select apps to block, and choose the movement required to earn minutes.

How are reps counted?

Kairox uses its camera-based movement experience to recognize supported exercises and count completed repetitions. Keep your body in frame and follow the positioning guidance shown in the app.

Which exercises are supported?

Kairox is designed around push-ups, squats, and lunges.

Why did an app remain blocked?

Check that required Screen Time or Family Controls permissions remain enabled, that earned minutes are available, and that the app is included in the active restriction.

Can I disable notifications?

Yes. Notification permissions and preferences can be changed in Kairox and in iOS Settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, select Kairox, and follow Apple’s cancellation options.
