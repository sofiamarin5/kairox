FAQ

The important stuff.

What does Kairox do?

It blocks selected distracting social apps and lets you earn controlled unlock time by completing supported exercises.

Which exercises are supported?

Kairox is designed around push-ups, squats, and lunges.

How are reps counted?

The exercise experience uses the camera to recognize supported movement and count completed repetitions.

Does it permanently block my apps?

No. You choose the apps and rules. Kairox is meant to replace automatic access with intentional, earned access.
